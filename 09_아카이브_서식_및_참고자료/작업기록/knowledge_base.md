# KNOWLEDGE BASE: Multiscale Active Inference & Biological Thermodynamics Framework of Suseung-hwagang (水昇火降)

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Theoretical Thesis
* **Title Domain:** Bio-philosophical and Neuro-thermodynamic Interpretation of *Suseung-hwagang* (水昇火降, Water-Ascending and Fire-Descending).
* **Core Paradigm Shift:** Reinterpreting *Suseung-hwagang* from a traditional doctrine of Yin–Yang regulation into a dynamical heuristic model. It articulates how living systems sustain organizational identity, adaptive capacity, and Non-Equilibrium Steady State (NESS) under continual environmental dissipation.
* **Pathological Counterpart:** *Hwaseung-sugang* (火昇水降, Fire-Ascending and Water-Descending) is formulated as the cosmological and thermodynamic image of dispersion, dissociation, gradient destruction, and unconstrained informational uncertainty ($\text{Surprise} / \text{Prediction Error}$).

## 2. Theoretical Axes: Four Pillar Integration

### 2.1 Autopoiesis (Francisco Varela & Humberto Maturana)
* **Organizational Closure:** The living system is operationally closed; its internal processes recursively generate the very components that sustain the process network.
* **Boundary & Epistemic Location:** Operational closure constructs an autopoietic boundary (Markov Blanket). The position of being unable to directly perceive external states ($\eta$) and having to infer them via sensory states ($s$) establishes the fundamental **First-Person Observer Perspective (Sense-making)**.
* **Life-Mind Continuity:** Living is cognition. Biological self-maintenance is intrinsically an active sense-making process.

### 2.2 Conatus (Baruch Spinoza)
* **Immanent Striving:** The intrinsic impulse of an entity to persevere in its own being and expand its power of acting.
* **FEP Mapping:** Mathematically formalized as the **Prior Preference Distribution $p(y)$** in Active Inference. Living entities possess an innate homeostatic expectation of the physiological bounds necessary for survival.

### 2.3 Non-Equilibrium Thermodynamics (Ilya Prigogine & Physics)
* **Thermodynamic Free Energy ($G$):** Material-energetic conditions. Organisms maintain local order by exploiting, transforming, and dissipating external free-energy gradients ($\Delta G$).
* **Entropic Dissipation:** Living systems continuously export internal entropy ($d_i S$) to the environment via external fluxes ($d_e S$), satisfying $\frac{dS_{\text{earth}}}{dt} \approx 0$ at NESS.

### 2.4 Variational Free Energy Principle (Karl Friston)
* **Variational Free Energy ($F$):** Informational-computational condition. An upper bound on surprise ($U = -\ln p(s)$) and prediction error ($\epsilon$).
* **Active Inference:** The dual loop of perception (updating internal generative models $\mu$) and action (modifying active states $a$ to alter sensory inputs $s$).
* **Multiscale Integration:** Markov blankets are nested (Matryoshka-like) from single molecules and cells to tissue, organ networks (NTS, PBN, Insula), and macro-subject consciousness.

## 3. Analytical Distinction of Two Free Energies

$$\text{Suseung-hwagang (水昇火降)} \iff \min F \quad \text{and} \quad \Delta G_{\text{dissipative optimization}}$$

1. **Thermodynamic Free Energy ($G$):**
   * *Domain:* Physical, metabolic, and chemical substrate level.
   * *Mechanism:* Nourishing *Su* (水, water/fluid) and descending *Hwa* (火, fire/heat) maintains physical gradients, cellular ATP consumption, and thermal dissipation.
2. **Variational Free Energy ($F$):**
   * *Domain:* Informational, inferential, and neuro-cognitive level.
   * *Mechanism:* Neural/interoceptive networks minimize prediction error ($\epsilon$) between top-down generative models and bottom-up sensory streams.
3. **Coupling Loop:** Successful minimization of $F$ (informational optimization) enables the organism to efficiently channel and dissipate $G$ (physical survival) without entropic collapse.

## 5. Neurobiology of Interoception & Anxiety Pathology

### 5.1 Interoceptive Inference Axis
* **Subcortical Nodes:** Nucleus Tractus Solitarius (NTS) and Parabrachial Nucleus (PBN) collect visceral/autonomic inputs (*Su-gi*, 水氣).
* **Cortical Integration Node:** Anterior Insula (aIns) acts as the primary hub translating interoceptive signals into 1st-person feeling states and homeostatic predictions (*Hwa-gi*, 火氣 control).

### 5.2 Anxious Brain Mechanics (Jelinčić & von Leupoldt, 2021; Harrison et al., 2021)
1. **Aberrant Precision Weighting:** Over-weighting top-down aversive priors creates an influx of false prediction errors ($\epsilon$).
2. **Interoceptive Dimensions Breakdown:**
   * **Objective Accuracy:** Decreases (e.g., impaired respiratory filter sensitivity).
   * **Subjective Sensibility:** Overfitted & catastrophized (high BPQ/BCS scores).
   * **Metacognitive Awareness:** Degrades (inability to distinguish benign vs. noxious bodily signals).
3. **Anterior Insula (aIns) De-differentiation:** 7T fMRI shows failure of aIns to differentiate benign vs. threatening interoceptive predictions.
4. **TKM Correlation:** Represents the neural reality of *Hwaseung-sugang* (火昇水降) / *Sin-su-bul-gyo* (心腎不交)—the dissociation between upper mental/sympathetic activation (*Hwa*) and lower visceral/vagal grounding (*Su*).

## 6. Mathematical Foundations & Formalisms

### 6.1 Prigogine Entropy Production Formula
$$\frac{dS_{\text{earth}}}{dt} = \frac{d_i S_{\text{earth}}}{dt} + \frac{d_e S_{\text{earth}}}{dt}$$
* Healthy State: $d_i S > 0$, $d_e S < 0$, and $|d_e S| \ge d_i S \implies \frac{dS}{dt} \approx 0$
* Global Warming / Pathological State: Entropy export $|d_e S|$ is blocked $\implies \frac{dS}{dt} > 0$

### 6.2 Kleiber's Law & Metabolic Scaling
$$B = B_0 M^{3/4} \implies \frac{B}{M} \propto M^{-1/4}$$
* Specific Entropy Production Rate: $\sigma = \frac{1}{M} \frac{d_i S}{dt} \propto M^{-1/4}$
* Multicellularity scales mass ($M$), drastically reducing mass-specific entropy production rate ($\sigma$), enabling complex structural order.

### 6.3 Helmholtz Decomposition of NESS Flow
Langevin dynamics under FEP:
$$\dot{x} = -\Gamma \nabla U(x) + Q \nabla U(x) + \omega$$
* **$-\Gamma \nabla U(x)$ (Irrotational / Gradient Flow):** Dissipative movement towards surprise potential $U(x) = -\ln p(x)$ (Waddington Landscape).
* **$Q \nabla U(x)$ (Solenoidal / Rotational Flow):** Non-equilibrium circulatory flux maintained by energy consumption (ATP/Gradient usage), breaking detailed balance.

### 6.4 Algebraic Closure of Autopoiesis
$$\forall a, b \in S \implies a \circ b \in S$$
* Operational closure is isomorphic to algebraic closure: all processes of production within system $S$ generate components that remain within $S$, forming a recursive causal loop ($f(x) = x$).
